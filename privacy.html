Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to "TikTok Scheduler" (the "App"), a personal software tool built and operated by John Bradley for his own individual use. The App is not a public service and does not collect information from, or about, any member of the public.

1. What information is processed

The App processes the following information, all belonging to the operator's own TikTok account:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used only to authenticate API requests to publish content to the operator's own account;
  • Basic account info returned by TikTok's API (such as display name and open id) to confirm which account is connected;
  • Video files, captions, and scheduling details the operator uploads through the App, for the sole purpose of posting them to TikTok at the chosen time.

2. Where information is stored

All of the above is stored locally on infrastructure controlled by the operator (a local database and file storage). Nothing is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. No advertising or analytics services are used, and no cookies are set for tracking purposes.

3. Retention and deletion

Video files and scheduling data are kept only as long as needed to publish them, and are removed from the queue once published or when the operator deletes them. OAuth tokens are kept only for as long as the App remains connected to the TikTok account, and are discarded if the connection is revoked.

4. Third parties

The only third-party service the App communicates with is TikTok itself, via its official Content Posting API, for the purpose of authenticating and publishing content to the operator's own account.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to review or delete stored data, can be sent to c.johnbradley@gmail.com.
